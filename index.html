About Me

I'm Suhas Palla, a first-year Industrial Engineering student at UIUC with a minor in Computer Science. I'm interested in the intersection of engineering, software, and design — whether that's building full-stack apps, training machine learning and AI models, or designing CAD components for hardware systems.

My project work includes Android development, NBA analytics platforms, and distracted driving detection using CNNs — all built with real users and real data in mind. On the engineering side, I design CAD housing components for pneumatic systems with ASME and consult on AI-driven logistics problems with ICON.

I've also taught coding and robotics to 50+ students as a STEM camp leader, which gave me a deep appreciation for communicating technical ideas clearly. I'm always looking for projects where engineering meets software — feel free to reach out.
