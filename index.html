Sheet 01 / About

The short version

I'm a second-year Industrial Engineering student at UIUC with a CS minor. Most of what I do lands in one of two buckets: designing physical hardware or writing software. The projects I actually care about need both.

On the hardware side I design drivetrain and carbon-fiber parts for Illini EV Concept, reverse-engineered a quadcopter (and ran the FEA to prove the leg wouldn't snap), and I'm redesigning a stroke-recovery robot with ASME.

On the software side I lead app development for FiPet — a financial-literacy app that's actually on the App Store — built a property-search site handling 53K listings over the summer, and trained a CNN to catch distracted drivers.

Right now I'm after internships where both halves matter. If that sounds like something you've got, my email's at the bottom.
